Turn on notifications

iOS only allows notifications for apps added to your Home Screen — not for pages open in Safari itself.

1. Tap the Share icon in Safari
2. Choose "Add to Home Screen"
3. Open Sakura from that new icon, then turn reminders on from there

Sign out?

Deleting a task or entry here always gives you a few seconds to undo it — signing out doesn't have that safety net, so this asks first instead.

Sakura Hub

The mobile companion to Sakura's full outliner — To-Dos and Journal, built around mobile-native gestures (swipe, tap, undo-toast) instead of a shrunk desktop layout.

Requires signing in (Google or email), unlike the desktop app — a phone's local storage never crosses devices on its own, so an account is what actually bridges this page and the desktop one. Meeting Notes, Library, Recap, AI features, and everything else stay desktop-only for now.

Signing in with Google only shares your name, email, and profile picture; email sign-in needs just the email and password themselves. To-Dos and Journal entries sync through the same account as the desktop app — nothing here is used for anything beyond that sync, no analytics or tracking. Manage or delete your account from the full app on desktop.
